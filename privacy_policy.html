Introduction

Welcome to Three Win: Tic Tac Toe ("the Application"), developed by Prem Kalyan ("Developer", "We", "Us", or "Our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Three Win: Tic Tac Toe is a lightweight, distraction-free strategy board game built for Android devices, supporting Single Player (vs AI) and Two Player local offline gameplay modes across 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5 grid sizes.

The Application is built with a privacy-by-design, offline-first architecture: it requires no user account creation, no registration, and no network connectivity to enjoy the full game experience. This Privacy Policy transparently explains our data practices in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google Play User Data Policy, and global privacy standards including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and COPPA.

2

Information We Collect & Local Storage

Three Win: Tic Tac Toe follows a strict principle of data minimization. We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from any user.

🔒 Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, biometric data, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers. You can play Three Win: Tic Tac Toe with 100% anonymity.

How Gameplay Preferences & Statistics Are Saved:

The Application saves your personal preferences and game records locally on your Android device using Android's standard SharedPreferences private sandbox:

  • Game Statistics (game_stats): Total counts of wins, losses, and draws across matches.
  • User Settings (game_settings): Vibration toggle state, preferred player mark (X or O), and selected UI color theme.

This data is stored solely on your physical device for the purpose of preserving your settings between sessions. It is never uploaded to any remote server or shared with any third party. You can clear this data at any time by tapping the "Reset Stats" button in the app's settings or by clearing the app storage in your Android device settings.

3

Device Permissions

Three Win: Tic Tac Toe declares only one standard Android permission in its application manifest:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE (Protection Level: Normal)
📳 Purpose of the VIBRATE Permission

This permission is used strictly to provide short tactile haptic feedback (vibrations) when tapping on cells and interacting with game controls. No data is collected, stored, or transmitted through the use of this permission. Users can turn haptic vibration on or off at any time in the in-game Settings dialog.

The Application does NOT request any dangerous or privacy-sensitive permissions, such as Internet access, Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, Phone State, or External Storage.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

We believe in a clean, distraction-free gaming environment:

  • No Advertisements: Three Win: Tic Tac Toe contains zero third-party ads, ad banners, interstitial ads, or rewarded video ad networks.
  • No Tracking / Analytics SDKs: Three Win does not integrate or run any third-party telemetry, analytics, or behavioral tracking SDKs.

The Application operates independently on your device and does not share data with any commercial data brokers or advertising exchanges.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Protecting children's privacy is of the utmost importance. Three Win: Tic Tac Toe is a family-friendly board game designed for users of all ages, including children under 13.

In full accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not collect any personal information from children or any other users.
  • We do not display targeted or behavioral advertisements.
  • We do not require account registration or social media logins.
  • We do not request sensitive device permissions.
6

Data Security & Architecture

Because Three Win: Tic Tac Toe stores all preferences and game records strictly on your local device without transmitting data over the internet, your game records are safeguarded by Android's hardware-level application sandboxing architecture.

Operating completely offline eliminates traditional cloud server risks such as data breaches, unauthorized server access, or network eavesdropping.

7

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Privacy regulations such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) provide users with rights regarding their personal information, such as rights to access, delete, or port data.

🛡️ Direct User Control

Because Three Win: Tic Tac Toe collects and stores zero personal data on any server, there is no remote user data to access, rectify, or delete from our systems. You maintain 100% direct control over your on-device data:

  • Clear Stats In-App: Tap the "Reset Stats" button in the in-game Settings or Game Screen at any time.
  • Clear App Data via Android: Navigate to Android Settings → Apps → Three Win → Storage → Clear Data to wipe all local settings immediately.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app completely erases all local SharedPreferences stored by the application.
